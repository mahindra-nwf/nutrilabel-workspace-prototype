# Ideas log

New concepts the Workspace design needs. One line each, with why it matters to chefs.

- **Folders (nested)**: chefs group work the way the kitchen does (Soups › Winter Specials) instead of one long list.
- **My recipes vs Shared with me**: what I own and must look after is kept apart from what teammates handed me to check.
- **Recent**: most days start with "the recipe I was on yesterday"; this gets them there in one click.
- **Stars**: the 5–10 house basics (vinaigrette, stocks) everyone reuses stay one click away.
- **Archive instead of delete**: R&D never truly throws a formula away; archived recipes come back with Restore, and Undo covers a slip of the finger.
- **Sharing with roles (Can view / Can edit)**: a chef can let QA or sales look without letting them change the formula.
- **Sharing a folder shares everything in it**: share "Frozen Meals" once instead of 20 recipes one by one.
- **Everyone at Northwest Frozen can view**: house standards are readable by the whole company without managing a list.
- **Card numbers (kcal / portion, cost / portion)**: the two numbers chefs check first, visible without opening the recipe.
- **"Edited 2 h ago by Ana"**: shows at a glance who touched a recipe last, so you know who to ask.
- **Tags on the card strip**: quick tells like "vegan" or "contains nuts" without opening anything; search matches tags too.
- **Colored thumbnail strip**: recipes are easier to spot by color than by reading names in a busy kitchen.
- **Drag a recipe onto a folder (or a breadcrumb)**: tidying feels like moving paper between trays.
- **Duplicate**: most new recipes start as "the old one, but…"; the copy is owned by you and starts unshared.
- **Activity log per item**: answers "who changed this and when" before a label goes to print.
- **List view with sortable columns**: R&D comparing costs or calories across a folder wants numbers lined up, not cards.
- **Details panel (ⓘ)**: check a recipe's numbers, owner and recent changes without leaving the list.
- **Undo on move and remove-access**: one wrong drag or click shouldn't mean hunting for where a recipe went.
- **Move to… with "New folder here"**: tidying a messy list usually needs a folder that doesn't exist yet.
- **View-only folders greyed out in Move to…**: makes it obvious why you can't drop a recipe into someone else's folder.
- **Open goes straight into the Recipe Builder, one tab per recipe**: chefs move from "find it" to "work on it" in one step, and come back to the same tab next time.
- **Numbers on cards come from the builder (later)**: kcal and cost on the card should be the live totals, so nobody trusts a stale number.
- **Comments (later)**: a QA note or tasting note attached to the recipe instead of lost in email. Out of scope for the prototype.
